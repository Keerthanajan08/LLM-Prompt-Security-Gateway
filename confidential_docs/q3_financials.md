# Q3 Financial Summary (CONFIDENTIAL, fabricated demo data)

Q3 revenue came in at $41.2M, down 12% quarter over quarter, driven mainly by the loss of two large enterprise customers, Altamira Logistics and Brightwell Health, who moved to a competitor at renewal.

Gross margin fell from 71% to 64% because cloud hosting costs rose 18% while revenue declined, and the discounts given to retain mid-market accounts averaged 22%.

The board has approved a hiring freeze for Q4 and a reduction of roughly 9% of headcount, concentrated in the sales and customer success organisations. The announcement is planned for November 14.

Cash runway is 19 months at the current burn rate of $3.1M per month. Finance recommends raising a bridge round before the end of Q1 if Q4 revenue does not recover above $43M.

We expect to miss the full-year revenue guidance of $180M given to investors in February, and the revised internal forecast is $166M.
